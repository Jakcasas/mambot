MAMBOT 1.1 - WINDOWS 64-BIT
========================

1. Bam chuot phai Mambot 1.1.zip, chon Extract All / Giai nen tat ca.
2. Mo thu muc Mambot 1.1 da giai nen, KHONG chay CMD ngay trong cua so ZIP.
3. Bam dup START_MAMBOT.cmd. Trinh duyet tu mo khi may chu san sang.
4. Giu cua so may chu mo trong khi dung. Ctrl+C de dung.

NEU BAN TAI mambot-main.zip TU NUT CODE TREN GITHUB:
- ZIP do chi co ma nguon, khong kem runtime/python.
- START_MAMBOT.cmd se tu tai goi Windows chinh thuc, kiem tra SHA-256 va chay.
- May can Internet trong lan dau. Goi Windows truc tiep:
  https://github.com/Jakcasas/mambot/releases/download/v1.1.0/Mambot.1.1.zip

Khong can cai Python, Node.js, MongoDB hay tai thu vien qua Internet.
Goi Windows x64 kem Python 3.12 va cac thu vien can thiet.
Chat co san va kho tri thuc chay offline; AI ngoai va website HUIT can mang.

Mac dinh: http://127.0.0.1:8000/mambot/
Neu cong 8000 ban, chuong trinh thu 8001-8009 va in dia chi da chon.
Neu trinh duyet khong tu mo, copy dia chi tu cua so Mambot.
Neu dat PORT thu cong, chuong trinh chi dung cong do.

NEU VAN LOI
- Chay KIEM_TRA_MAMBOT.cmd va doc thong bao PASS/LOI.
- Bao thieu runtime: giai nen LAI TOAN BO ZIP vao thu muc moi.
- Khong chi chep START_MAMBOT.cmd; giu nguyen runtime/backend/static/data/registry.
- Khong can thu muc .venv cu. Khong ho tro Windows 32-bit.
- macOS/Linux: dung ma nguon va huong dan cai thu vien trong HUONG_DAN.md.

Xem HUONG_DAN.md de cau hinh AI va phat trien ma nguon.
